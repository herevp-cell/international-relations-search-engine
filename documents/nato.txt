NATO Update

NATO is a political and military alliance of countries in Europe and North America. Its main purpose is to protect the freedom and security of its members through political and military cooperation.

NATO was founded in 1949. The alliance is based on collective defence, meaning that an attack against one member is considered an attack against all members.

NATO cooperates with its members on defence, security, military planning, crisis management and international cooperation. The organization also works with partner countries on security-related issues.

NATO remains an important international organization in European and global security.
